About Me

I didn't set out to be "a data person" and "a builder" at the same time—it just happened that way.

I'm Christina Martina Afum, a dedicated professional committed to excellence and continuous growth. With a focus on innovation, strategic thinking, and impactful results, I bring a unique blend of skills and experience to every endeavor.

My approach emphasizes quality, attention to detail, and a deep commitment to professional excellence. I believe in the power of collaboration, creativity, and taking initiative to drive meaningful change.

Featured Project: UCC-SAMS

My capstone project is a full staff attendance management system for the university with GPS geofencing, face recognition, fingerprint fallback, six levels of role-based access, and an offline-first design so it keeps working even when the internet doesn't. Built as a progressive web app using Lovable AI with a Supabase backend, mapping onto UCC's entire institutional hierarchy.

Outside the spreadsheets and the SQL scripts, I organize community events—because data work is better when it doesn't crowd out the rest of life. I go by Ms_CMA online: just a tech girl figuring things out in public, one project at a time.
